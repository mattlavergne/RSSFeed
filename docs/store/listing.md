## Detailed description (paste into "Description")

Tributary is a fast, private RSS reader that lives in your browser toolbar. No account, no subscription, no limits — every feature is free.

★ READ YOUR WAY
• Quick popup reader or a full-tab, three-pane reader
• Unread, starred, folder and per-feed views, plus search across all your posts
• Load the full article for feeds that only include a summary
• Podcasts and videos play right in the reader
• Light and dark themes, adjustable text size, compact or comfortable list
• Keyboard shortcuts: j/k to move, s to star, m to mark read, o to open, / to search

★ FOLLOW ANY SITE
• Works with RSS, Atom and JSON Feed
• Paste a website address and Tributary finds its feeds for you
• The toolbar icon shows a + when the page you're on has a feed — one click to subscribe
• Right-click any page or link → Subscribe
• Unlimited feeds, refreshed as often as every minute
• Private feeds with a username and password are supported

★ STAY ON TOP OF WHAT MATTERS
• Desktop notifications for new posts, per feed
• Keyword alerts: get notified when a post mentions the words you care about
• Filters: automatically highlight, star, mark as read, or hide posts by keyword or pattern
• Unread count on the toolbar icon

★ YOUR DATA, YOUR WAY
• Import and export OPML — move from or to any other RSS reader, folders included
• Full backup and restore (feeds, settings, rules and starred posts)
• Sync your subscriptions, folders and rules between computers with Chrome sync
• Everything else stays on your device. No tracking, no ads, no analytics.

Tributary is open source.
