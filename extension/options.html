About

Tributary is a free, open-source RSS, Atom and JSON Feed reader that runs entirely in your browser.

  • No account, no server, no tracking, no limits on feeds.
  • Feeds are fetched directly from your browser; data is stored locally (IndexedDB).
  • Subscriptions can optionally sync through your Chrome profile.

Keyboard shortcut to open the popup: Alt+Shift+R — change shortcuts.
